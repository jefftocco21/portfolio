    Overall Project Approach

    I used a hybrid approach for this project. Since I needed to watch a tutorial to re-learn Laravel, the requirements for the project followed a largely predetermined product backlog, with respect to both the definition of and priority of the activities. I therefore created a work breakdown structure that followed a predictive approach, but organized the actual completion of the activities into sprints that made sense with my schedule, which can be seen by referencing the Gantt chart as well as snippets from my Jira project. Even though many of the requirements and scope were specifically designated ahead of time to follow a tutorial, I still valued sprint planning, backlog refinement, and reviews with which I consulted some peers for feedback. I also did sprint retrospectives, which I found to have value regardless of the fact that the endeavor was completed by me as an individual.

     

    TLDR: After learning about both predictive and adaptive methodologies, I found that using elements from both for this project worked best for me.
